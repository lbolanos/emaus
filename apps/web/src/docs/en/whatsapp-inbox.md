# Retreat WhatsApp

The inbox where the team sees the messages walkers — and their emergency
contacts — send by WhatsApp to the staff's phones (logistics, palanqueros,
coordination).

📺 **[Watch the video tutorial](https://youtu.be/_SLDmmOjTPo)**

## What it does

- **Consolidated**: whatever arrives on *any* linked phone is seen by the whole
  authorized retreat team — even if the walker texted another member's number.
- **Read-only**: nothing is ever sent from the system; you reply from your own
  WhatsApp with the **Reply from my WhatsApp** button.
- **No blue check**: reading a message in the app never notifies the walker.
- **Only during the retreat**: it is enabled retreat by retreat and, once it
  ends, everything is deleted automatically (sessions closed, history purged
  after 15 days).
- **Emergency contacts too**: the mother, the husband or whoever the walker
  listed as a contact often send the letters and sometimes the payments. Their
  messages are marked 👪 as "emergency contact of …". Only users who can see
  health data see them.

## How to use it

The inbox has two tabs: **Messages** (the inbox) and **Settings** (linked
phones and the superadmin controls).

1. **The superadmin enables the inbox** for the retreat with the
   **Inbox enabled for this retreat** switch, under **Settings** (it costs
   server memory per linked phone — that is why only they turn it on).
2. **Each logistics member links their WhatsApp**: under **Settings**, the
   **Link my WhatsApp** button → read the notice (what gets synced and the
   risks) → scan the QR code with your phone, just like one more WhatsApp Web
   session.
3. **The team reads the inbox**: under **Messages**, each walker is one row
   with their name, what you have not read and the time. If their emergency
   contacts wrote too, the row expands (▸) and shows each chat separately. Tap
   a message to view it larger.
4. **Reply**: the **Reply from my WhatsApp** button — opens the chat with that
   walker from your own number, as always.
5. **Receipts**: if the walker sends a photo of their deposit, the
   **Register as payment** button downloads the image and opens the payment
   form with the receipt attached and the participant prefilled.

## Inbox tools

- **Search messages**: search any word; accents are ignored ("sabana" finds
  "sábana").
- **Unread only** and **Mark all as read** to bring the inbox back to zero.
- The walker's profile (phones, bed, email) opens from the ⓘ next to their
  name in the thread.
- **Photos, documents, videos and voice notes**: photos show in the thread and
  a voice note plays with **Listen** (if it does not play on iPhone, open it
  from your WhatsApp). Tapping a document or a video opens it big inside the
  inbox when the browser can show it (PDF page by page, image, text, video).
  Word, PowerPoint, Excel and the like come down with **Download and open** and
  open with your device's application; on a phone, with its preview.
- For safety, the inbox **does not open** documents with macros (.docm,
  .xlsm…) or files such as .html, .svg, .zip or programs: it tells you why and,
  if you trust the sender, you open it from your WhatsApp.
- **Register as payment**: from a photo or a PDF (its first page is used). The
  message is marked "Payment registered by …" and, if someone tries to register
  it again, the inbox asks first. The receipt also shows under **Payments →
  Receipts → Applied**.
- **Add as letter**: the message text (editable) and its image join the letters
  of the participant you choose, ready to print with the others. If the family
  sent the letter as a Word file or a PDF, the original document is kept with
  the letter (in the participant's folder, to open or print separately).
- **Awaiting reply / … replied**: the list shows whether someone on the team
  already answered that walker, so nobody answers twice.
- **Reply with a template** opens the message with the retreat's templates; it
  is logged in the walker's history. It is not offered in an emergency
  contact's chat: reply from your WhatsApp.
- **Add a contact's WhatsApp**: the ✏ pencil next to the name in the thread
  edits the walker's cell phone and their two emergency contacts' cell phones.
  If the mother writes from a number that was not on record, add it there:
  within a minute her chat shows up, with what she had already written.
- **See it bigger**: drag the line between the list and the conversation to
  give more room to either (double-click puts it back), or use **Expand
  conversation** (⤢) to hide the list. The inbox remembers how you left it.

## Privacy and care

- Linking your number is **voluntary**. Nobody is required to.
- During the retreat window, messages arriving at your account go through the
  server; **only messages from this retreat's walkers and their emergency
  contacts are stored** (plus your replies, so the team knows you already
  answered), deleted when you unlink or 15 days after the retreat.
- If an emergency contact is someone in your family, your chat with that
  person also enters the inbox while you are linked.
- Your personal number is **never shown** to other team members.
- We use an unofficial, read-only WhatsApp client: the risk of your number
  being suspended is low but not zero — that is why it is disclosed before
  linking and why each person decides.

## Quick questions

- **Do messages reach the inbox without me linking anything?** No: the inbox
  is fed by the linked phones.
- **Does the walker see that I read their message?** No: the system never
  marks "read".
- **Can I send from the inbox?** No: sending is always from your own
  WhatsApp, with the reply button.
- **How many phones can be linked?** As many as the superadmin allows (each
  connection costs server memory).
