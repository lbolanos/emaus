# Retreat WhatsApp

The inbox where the team sees the messages walkers send by WhatsApp to the
staff's phones (logistics, palanqueros, coordination).

📺 **[Watch the video tutorial](https://youtu.be/_SLDmmOjTPo)**

## What it does

- **Consolidated**: whatever arrives on *any* linked phone is seen by the whole
  authorized retreat team — even if the walker texted another member's number.
- **Read-only**: nothing is ever sent from the system; you reply from your own
  WhatsApp with the **Reply from my WhatsApp** button.
- **No blue check**: reading a message in the app never notifies the walker.
- **Only during the retreat**: it is enabled retreat by retreat and, once it
  ends, everything is deleted automatically (sessions closed, history purged
  after 15 days).

## How to use it

1. **The superadmin enables the inbox** for the retreat with the
   **Inbox enabled for this retreat** switch (it costs server memory per
   linked phone — that is why only they turn it on).
2. **Each logistics member links their WhatsApp**: the **Link my WhatsApp**
   button → read the notice (what gets synced and the risks) → scan the QR
   code with your phone, just like one more WhatsApp Web session.
3. **The team reads the inbox**: walkers' conversations appear with their
   name, what you have not read and the time. Tap a message to view it larger.
4. **Reply**: the **Reply from my WhatsApp** button — opens the chat with that
   walker from your own number, as always.
5. **Receipts**: if the walker sends a photo of their deposit, the
   **Register as payment** button downloads the image and opens the payment
   form with the receipt attached and the participant prefilled.

## Inbox tools

- **Search messages**: search any word; accents are ignored ("sabana" finds
  "sábana").
- **Unread only** and **Mark all as read** to bring the inbox back to zero.
- The walker's profile (phones, bed, email) opens from the ⓘ next to their
  name in the thread.
- **Photos, documents and voice notes**: photos show in the thread; a document
  opens with **Open document** and a voice note plays with **Listen** (if it
  does not play on iPhone, open it from your WhatsApp).
- **Register as payment**: from a photo or a PDF (its first page is used). The
  message is marked "Payment registered by …" and, if someone tries to register
  it again, the inbox asks first.
- **Add as letter**: the message text (editable) and its image join the letters
  of the participant you choose, ready to print with the others.
- **Awaiting reply / … replied**: the list shows whether someone on the team
  already answered that walker, so nobody answers twice.
- **Reply with a template** opens the message with the retreat's templates; it
  is logged in the walker's history.

## Privacy and care

- Linking your number is **voluntary**. Nobody is required to.
- During the retreat window, messages arriving at your account go through the
  server; **only this retreat's walkers' messages are stored** (plus your
  replies to those walkers, so the team knows you already answered), deleted
  when you unlink or 15 days after the retreat.
- Your personal number is **never shown** to other team members.
- We use an unofficial, read-only WhatsApp client: the risk of your number
  being suspended is low but not zero — that is why it is disclosed before
  linking and why each person decides.

## Quick questions

- **Do messages reach the inbox without me linking anything?** No: the inbox
  is fed by the linked phones.
- **Does the walker see that I read their message?** No: the system never
  marks "read".
- **Can I send from the inbox?** No: sending is always from your own
  WhatsApp, with the reply button.
- **How many phones can be linked?** As many as the superadmin allows (each
  connection costs server memory).
