# Retreat flyer

The flyer is the poster you share with the people who are going to sign up. It is built from the
retreat's own data — dates, venue, cost, contacts — and **Edit flyer** lets you change how it
looks without touching any of that data.

Everything you do here is saved **for this retreat only**. To reuse a design on the next one, save
it as a template.

## Moving and hiding blocks

The flyer is made of blocks: introduction, start, venue, end of the retreat, registration QR,
cost, contact and what to bring.

- **Drag them on the flyer** to move them around or across columns.
- With a keyboard or on a phone, use the **arrows** in the list on the Design tab.
- The **eye** hides a block. It stays in the list, so you can bring it back whenever you want.

An empty block is not drawn: if the retreat has no phone numbers or emails, the contact block does
not appear even when it is visible.

## Design

**Quick styles** change the whole flyer at once: Poster, Ink, Veils, and **Original**, which
brings the usual colours back. **Match colours to the image** reads the background photo and
suggests the palette that suits it — handy right after changing the image.

Inside, two sections you can fold away:

- **Whole flyer** — the text and heading colours for every block, the block background (none, a
  light or dark veil, or a colour with its opacity), and **dim the background image** when the
  photo competes with the words.
- **Blocks** — the list, and when you pick one, its own style: the same settings, for that block
  alone. **Reset** drops them and hands it back to the overall design.

Also per block: the **alignment** (left, centre or right). It moves more than the text — the
icon, the bullets and the boxes travel with it.

> ⚠️ The amber triangle warns that a text is hard to read against what is behind it. That is the
> mistake you do not notice on screen and do notice on paper. Try another text colour, a veil, or
> dimming the image.

## Images

You can change the body background, the header, the footer and the logo. For each one, pick one of
the images the app ships with or **upload your own** (it is resized and optimised for you).

Think about where the text will land: a high-contrast photo right behind a block is the most
common cause of an unreadable flyer.

## Texts

Every text on the flyer can be rewritten or taken off:

- Type over it to change it. Leave it **empty** and the default wording comes back.
- The **eye** takes it off the flyer entirely — for lines like "Retiro Católico" or "Atrévete,
  vívelo" that not every community uses. Show it again and your wording is still there.
- Line breaks you type are kept as they are on the flyer.

Remember the flyer is public: leave out the retreat's internal workings and any figures you would
rather not have circulating.

## Templates

**Save as template** keeps the design — blocks, images, colours, texts — under a name, so you can
apply it to another retreat. Save it for yourself or, if you administer a community, share it with
the people who administer it with you.

Each template's **eye** shows it applied to this retreat before you commit. Applying replaces the
current design, so it asks first; the dates, cost and contacts stay this retreat's own.

## When something goes wrong

- **Undo** (`⌘Z` or the button) steps back one change at a time. **Discard changes** throws away
  everything unsaved.
- If you leave with unsaved changes, the app warns you.
- Nothing here touches the retreat's data: if the flyer shows the wrong date, fix it on the
  retreat, not on the flyer.

## Sharing and printing

From the flyer's menu you can **print**, **copy the image** or **download the PDF**. It scales
itself to fit on one page, so hiding or moving blocks never leaves you adjusting anything.
