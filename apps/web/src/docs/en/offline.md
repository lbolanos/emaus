# Working without a connection at the retreat house

📺 **[Watch video tutorial](https://youtu.be/ICF-5spw920)**

Many retreat houses have a weak signal or none at all. The app keeps working on your phone with what it saved, and what you do without signal is sent automatically when the connection comes back.

## Before leaving: prepare the phone

On the **retreat dashboard** the **Work without a connection** card appears from one week before the retreat. Tap **Prepare to work without a connection** with a good signal (for example, on Thursday at home): the phone downloads walkers, servers, reception, beds, tables, minute by minute, responsibilities, Santísimo and service teams.

During the retreat the phone downloads again by itself every time you open the retreat with signal. For the app to also open without signal, use it at least once with a connection after logging in. On iPhone, use **Add to Home Screen** from Safari.

## What you can do without signal

- **Reception**: mark **Arrived**, **undo** an arrival, **charge** at the door and cancel who **did not arrive**.
- **Bags**: mark them as done or pending.
- **Minute by minute**: start and complete activities, move an activity **±5** minutes and move the whole day.
- **Beds**: assign, move and free a bed.
- **Tables**: assign a walker to a table and remove them.
- **Records**: correct first and last name, nickname, phones, parish, occupation, address, shirt size and snoring. The record says so when you open it. If you change any other field (the email, for example) nothing is saved and the notice names it; undo that change to save the rest. If somebody else changed that same field while there was no signal, your change is **rejected** with the reason and theirs is kept: check the record with signal.
- **Print**: the **badges**, every **Tables** sheet (the walker cards too) and the **minute by minute**. **Print Tables** comes out without medication, food, support needs or emergency contacts, and the sheet says so; if you need them on paper, print it with signal.

Everything else needs a connection. If you try it without signal, the app tells you that action needs signal; in Beds, **auto-assign**, **export** and **clear** are disabled, and so are **Download Tables (Word)** and the badges' **Export DOCX**.

## What you see without signal

- In the menu, under your name, the state: **Online**, **Offline** and how many **changes not sent** there are.
- At the top of each screen, a notice with the age of the saved data ("data saved 20 minutes ago").
- **Food** and **Medication** can only be checked with a connection: health data (medication, diets, notes and emergency contacts) is never saved on the phone.

## When the signal comes back

Changes are sent automatically, in the order you made them, with the **time they happened** (for example, the real time of each arrival). A payment is never recorded twice even if the sending is repeated.

Tap the state in the menu to see the list of **changes not sent**. If the server rejects one — for example, someone else already assigned that bed — it shows as **rejected** with the reason: you can **retry** after fixing it or **discard** it. A change is never lost unless you discard it.

If your session expired, you will see **Log in to send**: log in again and the changes are sent.

## Privacy

What is saved lives only on your phone and is deleted automatically when the retreat ends, when you log out, or with **Delete the data saved on this phone** from the list of changes. If you log out with changes not sent, they stay on the phone and are sent the next time you log in with your user.
