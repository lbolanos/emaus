# Responsibilities and Service Teams

📺 **[Watch the tutorial](https://youtu.be/3XZlQJZlcmo)** (Spanish)

These two screens organize the retreat's service: **Responsibilities** assigns tasks and talks to individual servers; **Service Teams** groups servers into teams (kitchen, music, palancas, logistics, etc.).

## Responsibilities

Each card is a responsibility or talk. You can:

- **Assign** a server to a responsibility, or **assign a speaker** to a talk (searching the system or entering them manually); remove the assignment with the X.
- View **documentation**, download support files (dynamics) and open **attachments** with each card's icons.
- In each card's **⋮** menu: **Edit**, **Delete** and, when a related team exists, **Add servers to team**.
- In the header's **⋮** menu: **Create a responsibility**, **Export to Word**, **Print** and, on a computer, the number of **columns**.
- Search and filter by **All / Talks / Responsibilities / Unassigned / By server**.

### Send tasks via WhatsApp (By server tab)

The **By server** tab lists each server with their responsibilities, how many teams
and documents they have, and **whether they already got their tasks**. From there you
send them a WhatsApp message listing **their responsibilities** and the **teams**
they belong to (as leader or member, with the other members of each team):

1. Open the **By server** tab. The header search filters by name, nickname or
   responsibility; **Not sent** leaves only the ones still pending.
2. Press **Send** on their row: the message opens already drafted with their tasks.
   Review and adjust it if needed.
3. **Enviar Mensaje** (Send message) opens the WhatsApp conversation with that server
   and the message written (if the browser can't open it, it copies it to the
   clipboard) and records the send in their history.
4. When the message closes you are back on the list: the row is marked **Sent on …**
   with the date and channel, and you move on to the next one. The button turns into
   **Resend**.

The message also includes the **documents** for what they do, with a link to open
them from the phone: those of their Minute by Minute moments (for example, the
palancas explanation or the Wall dynamic), those of their responsibilities and, for
table leaders and co-leaders, the table prayer dynamics. Only uploaded files travel
(PDF, Word, images); scripts written in the app have no link. Each server's row
shows how many documents they get.

The sent mark comes from the retreat's message history: every coordinator sees it,
and it stays after reloading the page or coming back another day.

If a server is not on the list, it is because they have no responsibility, team or
Minute by Minute moment assigned (or they are cancelled). Partial-support servers
(angelitos) are listed too.

## Service Teams

Each team has a type, a leader, and its members. You can:

- **Add a team** (choose the type and name) or **Create default teams** if none exist yet; delete them with confirmation.
- **Drag servers** between the "without team" lists and the team cards to assign or remove them.
- Search by team or by server, view each team's **instructions**, adjust columns, **export to Word**, and **print**.
