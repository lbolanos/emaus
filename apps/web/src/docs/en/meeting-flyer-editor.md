# Meeting Flyer

The meeting flyer has three fixed styles —Default, Poster, WhatsApp— and a fourth one,
**Custom**, that you design. From **Edit design** you move blocks around, change colours,
images and texts, just like in the retreat flyer editor.

Two things worth knowing before you start:

- **The design belongs to the community, not the meeting.** Everything you save here is inherited
  by every meeting of the community — same as the background from the previous screen. The photo,
  the title and the data are per meeting.
- The first time you open the editor it starts from the background the community had already
  chosen, so you don't start from scratch.

## Moving and hiding blocks

The flyer is made of five blocks: date and time, description, location, the location QR and the
community. The title and the header don't move: they are part of the frame.

- **Drag them on the flyer** to move them or cross columns.
- On the keyboard or on mobile, use the **arrows** in the Design tab list.
- The **eye** hides a block. It stays in the list, so you can bring it back whenever you want.

## Design

**Quick looks** change the whole flyer at once: Poster, Ink, Veils and **Original**, which
restores the starting point: clear text straight over the artwork, no cards. Applying a look
clears any cards you left on individual blocks; if you want cards, raise a block's background
opacity.

- **Whole flyer** — text and heading colours, the block backgrounds and **dimming the background
  image** when the photo competes with the letters.
- **Blocks** — the list, and on touching one (on the flyer or the list), its own style.

> ⚠️ The amber triangle warns that a text is barely readable over what's behind it. It is the
> mistake you don't notice on screen and do on paper. Try another text colour, a veil, or dimming
> the image.

The **QR** block keeps its fixed white plate: a QR over a photo doesn't always scan.

## Images

You can change the body background, the header background, the footer background and the logo.
For each you pick one of the images the app ships or **upload your own** (it gets resized and
optimised on its own).

## Texts

Every label on the flyer can be rewritten: the title, the top line, the date, duration,
description and location labels, the text under the QR and the footer.

- Type over it to change it. If you leave it **empty**, the default wording comes back.
- The **eye** removes it from the flyer entirely. When you show it again, you recover what you
  had written.

Remember the flyer is public: don't mention internal retreat dynamics or amounts you don't want
circulating.

## When something goes wrong

- **Undo** (`⌘Z` or the button) steps back one change at a time. **Discard changes** throws away
  everything unsaved.
- **Clear design** returns the community to the usual flyer — it asks first.
- If you leave with unsaved changes, the app warns you.
- The other three styles don't know about any of this: Default, Poster and WhatsApp keep coming
  out as always, with the community's background.

For now this editor has no templates; the retreat one does.

## Sharing and printing

From the flyer you can **print** or **copy the image**. It fits itself to one page, so hiding or
moving blocks never needs any manual adjusting.
