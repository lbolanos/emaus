# Special cases

When the palanquero talks to a walker before the retreat, they sometimes learn something the coordinator has to review: the walker needs a **separate room**, has a **health** condition, or some **other** personal situation. Special cases carry that heads-up from the palanquero to the coordinator without relying on scattered messages.

## Flagging a case (palanquero)

In **Palancas**, next to each walker's name there is a small flag. Tapping it opens a short dialog:

- **Category** (required): separate room, health, or other.
- **Note** (optional): what the coordinator needs to know, for example "uses a wheelchair" or "travels with their mother".

After saving, the row shows an amber tag with the category; hover over it to see the note.

## Attending the cases (coordinator)

On the **Retreat Dashboard**, the **Special cases** card shows how many are waiting and how many of each category. Tapping it (or the side menu, under **Communications → Special cases**) opens the inbox: name, phone, category, and note for each flagged walker, with a **Contact** button and a **Remove flag** button.

You handle it outside the system (call the walker, decide the room) and, once attended, **remove the flag**. Removing it erases the case's category and note; the walker's general notes are left untouched. The audit log keeps who flagged, with what reason, and who removed the flag.

## Who can see it

A special case is sensitive information, like the medical record: only profiles with the health-data permission see it (administration, treasury, logistics, and communications). Flagging or removing the flag also requires permission to edit participants.
